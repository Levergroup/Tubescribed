**TubeScribed vs Tactiq** comes down to the job you are hiring a tool for. Tactiq is built mainly for meetings: it captures transcripts in Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams, and it also offers a free tool that fetches a YouTube video's transcript. TubeScribed starts from a public YouTube URL, transcribes the audio, and turns it into a finished format such as a blog post, newsletter or training guide. If you need meeting notes, pick Tactiq. If you need content from YouTube videos, pick TubeScribed.

Below is a fair look at both, with Tactiq's plans read from its own site today, and a real 14-minute video we ran through TubeScribed, including what we had to fix.

## The short answer

- **Choose Tactiq** if your main source is live meetings, or if you only need a quick free transcript of a YouTube video to read or copy.
- **Choose TubeScribed** if the starting point is a public YouTube video and the goal is a usable draft: blog post, email, social posts, FAQ, SOP or training guide.
- **Use both** if you take meeting notes in Tactiq and also repurpose your published videos with TubeScribed. They do not overlap much.

Neither tool is better overall. Our earlier roundup of the [best YouTube transcript tools](/blog/best-youtube-transcript-tools-2026) reached the same conclusion: these tools sit in different parts of the workflow.

## What Tactiq does

Tactiq describes itself as a way to "transcribe, summarize, extract, and share meeting insights." Its Chrome extension works with Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams, and its navigation highlights real-time transcription with no bot joining the call. Features listed on its [pricing page](https://tactiq.io/buy) include AI summaries, in-meeting AI, and sharing or exporting transcripts.

Tactiq also has a separate [YouTube transcript guide](https://tactiq.io/learn/how-to-get-transcript-youtube-video). It describes a simple web flow: paste a YouTube URL into the tool, click "Get Video Transcript", then copy the text or download it as a .txt file. The guide says the tool is free and needs no email or installation.

A creator walkthrough on YouTube, [Irene Chan's Tactiq tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5WxSMovdT-U), shows how this fits together. She gets the transcript from Tactiq's free tool, uploads it into a Tactiq account, and then uses buttons for bullet-point highlights, a short summary, or a question about the video. That is a good fit if what you want is to understand or skim a long video.

## What TubeScribed does

You paste a public YouTube URL and TubeScribed transcribes the **audio**, so it works with or without captions. If audio recognition fails, it falls back to the video's captions and labels the result as caption-based. Videos can be up to 90 minutes long.

From the transcript you pick one of 15 output types: Blog Post, Email Newsletter, Social Posts, Brand SOP, Content Ideas, X Thread, Show Notes, FAQ Document, Key Takeaways, Slide Outline, Sales Script, Training Guide, Case Study, Press Release and Ad Copy. Brand workspaces save your tone, terminology and style so outputs sound like you. Pro includes 3 workspaces and Agency includes unlimited workspaces. Results download as .txt.

TubeScribed also works inside Claude as a [connector](/integrations/claude) and through a developer API.

## Pricing: what we read today

Tactiq's [pricing page](https://tactiq.io/buy), read today, lists these plans. Paid prices are shown per user, per month, with annual billing:

| Tactiq plan | Price | What the page lists |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0, no credit card | 10 transcripts a month, 5 AI credits a month |
| Pro | $8 | 10 AI credits a month, no cap on transcripts |
| Team | $16.67 | No cap on AI credits, priority support, auto-share transcripts |
| Business | $29.17 | Adds SAML SSO, a customer success manager and a beta MCP and Claude connector |
| Enterprise | Custom | For 200 or more users |

The page explains that one AI credit covers a single AI action, such as a summary, action items or a question to Tactiq AI. When you hit the limit, Tactiq keeps transcribing but AI features pause until the next month or until you upgrade.

TubeScribed prices differently. New users get free credits with no credit card. Pro is $19/month for 30 credits, and Agency is $49/month for 100 credits and 5 team members. Credit packs never expire: 10 for $8, 25 for $18 and 50 for $32. One credit covers up to 10 minutes of video (rounded up) or one AI output, and credits are only charged after success.

These are not like-for-like prices. Tactiq charges per user for meeting and AI features. TubeScribed charges by the video minute and by the output you generate. The cheaper tool depends on how many videos you process and how many people need seats.

## Real example: we ran a 14-minute video through TubeScribed

We picked a video that shows the manual workflow most people use after a transcript tool: [ChatGPT Demo: How To Repurpose Your Video Into A Blog Post Using ChatGPT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s1Mo9r1BKq8), from the Going Social channel. It runs 14 minutes 2 seconds.

We pasted the link into TubeScribed. Audio recognition did not succeed on this video, so TubeScribed fell back to the captions and labelled the transcript "from captions" (1,933 words). That cost 2 credits for the 14 minutes. We then asked for **Key Takeaways**, which cost 1 more, so 3 credits in total. The result was a 543-word document with a short summary, eight numbered actions and a closing note.

Here is a short excerpt of the real output:

> **Pivot quickly when the AI "goes off the rails."** If the AI fails to follow a specific instruction after two attempts, stop. Instead of repeating the same prompt, provide more explicit clarity or "reset" the instruction by pasting the successful sections back in and asking for the final missing piece.
>
> **Remember This:** The goal is not "one-shot" perfection; it is iterative refinement.

**What we had to fix:**

- **A misread number.** The summary said the method can "save 90% of your manual drafting and formatting time." In the video, the presenter says she gets "almost 90 percent of the way there," which is a different claim.
- **Added detail.** The output said the AI "automatically filters out verbal fillers." The presenter does not say that, so we cut it.
- **A reworded tip.** The output told readers to treat ChatGPT "as a human intern." The presenter calls it an assistant.
- **Caption limits.** Because the transcript was caption-based, any names or terms would have needed a check against the video.

Our verdict: the structure and the main lessons matched the video, and the takeaways were useful as a skim. It still needed a careful read against the source. Check every number in an AI draft, whichever tool wrote it.

## When Tactiq is the better choice

Be honest about the job. Choose Tactiq, or another meeting tool, if:

- **Your source is a meeting.** TubeScribed does not handle Google Meet, Zoom or Teams calls. Tactiq is built for them.
- **You want a quick, free transcript of a YouTube video.** For reading or copying the text, Tactiq's free tool is enough. YouTube's own **Show transcript** panel also works for videos that have captions, as [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15930243?hl=en) explains.
- **You want summaries and answers in a notes app.** Tactiq's AI actions run on transcripts inside your account.
- **Your video is not a public YouTube video, or is longer than 90 minutes.** TubeScribed works with public YouTube videos up to 90 minutes only.
- **You need per-seat meeting features.** Tactiq's Team and Business plans are designed for that.

## When TubeScribed is the better choice

TubeScribed fits when published video is a regular input to your content:

- You publish from webinars, coaching sessions, tutorials or podcasts every week.
- Some videos have no captions, or the auto-captions are poor.
- You want the output to be a draft, not a transcript you still have to prompt.
- You need a consistent brand voice across outputs or across clients.

If that sounds like your week, read our guide on turning a [YouTube transcript into a blog post](/blog/youtube-transcript-to-blog-post), and the full system in our [complete guide to repurposing YouTube content](/blog/repurpose-youtube-content). If your current workflow is a transcript plus a general chatbot, our comparison of [TubeScribed vs ChatGPT](/blog/tubescribed-vs-chatgpt) covers that route.

## The bottom line on TubeScribed vs Tactiq

Tactiq is a meeting tool with a handy free YouTube transcript tool attached. TubeScribed is a YouTube-to-content tool with no meeting features. The TubeScribed vs Tactiq choice is mostly about where your source material lives. If it lives in meetings, Tactiq is the natural pick. If it lives on YouTube and you publish from it, TubeScribed removes the copy, paste and prompt steps.
